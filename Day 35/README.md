## 🎯 Challenge Objective

Learn to create better prompts by combining:

- 👤 Role
- 📝 Task

- 🎨 Tone
- 📏 Constraints
- ✅ Desired Output

instead of writing vague instructions.

---

# 🧩 What I Learned

## 1️⃣ Role-Based Prompting

Assigning a role gives the AI context before the task.

Example:

> Act as a social media copywriter for a sustainable lifestyle brand.

This immediately improves relevance and writing style.

---

## 2️⃣ Clear Task Definition

Instead of:

> Write something about a water bottle.

Use:

> Write an Instagram caption announcing our new eco-friendly water bottle.

Specific tasks produce better responses.

---

## 3️⃣ Tone Matters

Adding instructions like:

- Fun
- Friendly
- Professional
- Persuasive

helps AI match the intended audience.

Example:

> Use a fun, energetic tone with 2–3 relevant emojis.

---

## 4️⃣ Constraints Improve Output

Good prompts include limitations.

Example:

- Under 150 characters
- End with a call-to-action
- Include 2–3 emojis

Constraints create focused responses.

---

# 💡 Prompt Engineering Principle

A strong prompt follows this structure:

```
Role
→ Task
→ Context
→ Tone
→ Constraints
→ Desired Output
```

The more specific the prompt, the more accurate and useful the AI response becomes.

---

# ✨ Example Optimized Prompt

> Act as a social media copywriter for a sustainable lifestyle brand. Write an Instagram caption announcing our new eco-friendly water bottle. Use a fun, energetic tone with 2–3 relevant emojis. Keep it under 150 characters and end with a clear call-to-action.
