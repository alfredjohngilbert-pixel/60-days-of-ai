🧠 Day 45 – AI Decision Strategist

An AI-powered decision-making assistant that transforms difficult choices into structured, logical, and actionable reports.

🚀 Project Overview

Making important life decisions can be overwhelming. This project uses Claude AI to guide users through a structured decision-making framework by comparing multiple options, identifying hidden assumptions, evaluating risks, and generating a personalized recommendation.

For this challenge, I explored the decision:

B.Tech Computer Science vs. BBA

Instead of giving a simple answer, the AI analyzed both choices from multiple perspectives and created a complete decision report.

✨ Features
📊 Decision Matrix with weighted scoring
⚖️ Compare multiple options
💡 Strengths & weaknesses analysis
🧠 Assumption Buster
⚠️ Premortem (What could go wrong?)
📅 7-Day action plan
🏆 AI-generated final verdict
📱 Shareable insight cards
🌙 Modern responsive dark UI

💡 What I Learned

This project taught me that good decisions are not about finding the "perfect" option.

Instead, they come from:

Breaking assumptions
Identifying biases
Comparing options objectively
Thinking about long-term alignment instead of short-term emotions

AI becomes a thinking partner rather than just an answer generator.
