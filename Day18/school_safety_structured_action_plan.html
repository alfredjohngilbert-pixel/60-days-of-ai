📝 Summary

✅ Decisions Made

Visitor Management System: Mandatory ID verification and visitor passes for all individuals entering campus, including parents during school hours.
Student Supervision Enhancement: Additional staff assignment during recess and transition periods; permission slips required for classroom departures during instructional hours.
Student Release Protocol: Digital checkout system implemented with parent/guardian verification before student release; security personnel cross-check records at gate.
Transportation Safety: Regular bus inspections, attendance checks during travel, and additional training for drivers and attendants on safety protocols.
Emergency Preparedness: Monthly emergency drills and safety awareness sessions for students and staff.
Implementation Timeline: All measures to be implemented immediately with official circular from Principal.
