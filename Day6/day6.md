# Day 6 – Resume Optimization with Claude AI

## Objective

Learn how to use Claude AI to analyze and optimize a resume for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and improve recruiter visibility.

---

## Original ATS Score

**Previous Score:** 52/100

### Challenges Identified

* ATS-unfriendly visual elements (icons, images, multi-column layout)
* Non-standard section headings
* Weak action verbs
* Limited keyword optimization
* Lack of measurable achievements
* Contact information not optimized for ATS parsing

---

## Optimized ATS Score

**Optimized Score:** 88/100

### Improvement

**+36 Points Increase**

---

## Key Changes Made

### 1. Removed Visual Elements

* Eliminated icons, images, and multi-column layouts.
* Converted resume into a single-column ATS-friendly format.

### 2. Standardized Section Headings

Used common ATS-recognized headings:

* PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY
* EDUCATION
* EXPERIENCE
* SKILLS
* LANGUAGES

### 3. Strengthened Action Verbs

Replaced passive wording with stronger action verbs:

* Assisted → Managed
* Observed → Coordinated
* Helped → Developed

### 4. Expanded Experience Descriptions

Added measurable outcomes and responsibilities.

Example:

* Coordinated administrative operations for 2,000+ student records.

### 5. Improved Keyword Optimization

Added recruiter-friendly keywords such as:

* International Business
* Business Operations
* Organizational Management
* Leadership Development
* Administrative Operations
* ChatGPT
* Claude AI
* Grok AI

### 6. Optimized Contact Placement

Placed:

* Name
* Phone Number
* Email
* Location
* LinkedIn

directly below the header for better ATS parsing.

## Key Learnings

1. ATS systems prefer simple single-column layouts.
2. Standard section headings improve resume parsing.
3. Strong action verbs increase recruiter engagement.
4. Keywords significantly affect ATS rankings.
5. Quantifiable achievements strengthen resume impact.
6. AI tools like Claude can quickly optimize resumes for job applications.

Successfully improved ATS compatibility from **52/100 to 88/100**, making the resume more searchable, recruiter-friendly, and optimized for modern hiring systems.
