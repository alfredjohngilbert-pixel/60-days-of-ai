Wrap-upWorkflow Summary

This roadmap takes an EdTech idea through ten disciplined stages: validating a real problem, sizing the market, choosing a defensible business model, forming the company correctly, shipping a lean MVP, building a brand that parents and institutions trust, acquiring your first paying users, becoming fundable, hiring a small operating team, and finally building the systems that let you scale without you personally holding everything together. AI tools compress research, drafting, design, and analysis time at every stage — but judgment on positioning, pricing, and who to trust with your first cheque remains entirely yours.
