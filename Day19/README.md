# Family, School & Business Intelligence Hub

An AI-powered assessment system that analyzes personal growth across three key areas:

- Family Intelligence
- School Success
- Business Growth

The system generates:

✅ Intelligence Reports  
✅ Success Predictions  
✅ Personal Intelligence Score  
✅ Success Archetype Analysis  
✅ Leadership & Growth Insights  
✅ Personalized Recommendations

---

## Features

### Stage 0: Experience Level Check
Determines the user's experience level and adjusts explanations accordingly.

### Stage 1: Intelligence & Success Prediction Report

Analyzes:

- Performance Metrics
- KPIs
- Goals
- Habits
- Strengths
- Weaknesses

Generates:

- Family Intelligence Report
- School Success Report
- Business Growth Report

---

### Stage 2: Personal Intelligence Quiz

Evaluates:

- Communication
- Leadership
- Learning Habits
- Time Management
- Decision Making
- Growth Mindset

Outputs:

- Personal Intelligence Score (0-100)
- User Classification
- Improvement Opportunities

---

### Stage 3: Personality & Success Style Assessment

Measures:

- Leadership
- Discipline
- Creativity
- Teamwork
- Resilience
- Ambition

Assigns:

- Visionary Leader
- Strategic Planner
- Growth Builder
- Reliable Achiever

---
