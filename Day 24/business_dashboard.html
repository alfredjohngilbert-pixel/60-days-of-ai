Sustainability Verdict

This business has a strong foundation because it already operates a successful school with a trusted brand and an established parent community. The Parent Connect platform should be used to improve retention, referrals, and operational efficiency rather than as a standalone product. If execution focuses on measurable outcomes and gradual digital expansion, the business has excellent long-term sustainability.
