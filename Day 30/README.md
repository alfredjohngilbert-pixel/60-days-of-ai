# 🚚 Supply Chain Builder Simulator

An interactive browser-based simulator that teaches the fundamentals of supply chain management through five strategic business decisions.

Built as **Day 30** of the **#60DayClaudeChallenge**.

# 🎯 Objective

Build the most effective supply chain by balancing:

- 💰 Cost Efficiency
- ⚡ Delivery Speed
- ⚠️ Risk
- 😊 Customer Satisfaction
- 🌱 Sustainability

Every decision changes your business performance.

---

# 🧩 Features

- Interactive 5-step simulation
- Random company generation
- Live KPI dashboard
- Real-time score updates
- Final Supply Chain Scorecard
- Personalized improvement suggestions
- Responsive UI
- Educational explanations for every decision

---

# 🛠 Technologies

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

---

# 📊 Business Metrics

The simulator tracks:

- Cost Efficiency
- Delivery Speed
- Risk Level
- Customer Satisfaction
- Sustainability

---

# 🎮 Decisions

The player chooses:

1. Supplier Strategy
2. Factory Location
3. Warehouse Strategy
4. Transportation Method
5. Inventory Strategy

Every choice affects the final score.
