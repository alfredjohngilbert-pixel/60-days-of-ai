Welcome

Why Media Integrity Matters

Every day, headlines and posts compete for your attention — and some of them bend the truth to get it. Media literacy isn't about distrusting everything. It's about noticing the small signals — word choice, framing, emotional pressure — that separate honest reporting from manipulation. In this lesson, you'll observe two real-feeling scenarios, form your own judgment first, then reveal the analysis behind it.
