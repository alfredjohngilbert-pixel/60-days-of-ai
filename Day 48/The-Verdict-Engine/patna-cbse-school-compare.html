Search approach

Each school was researched individually across its official fee-structure pages (where published), aggregator profiles (SchoolMyKids, Careers360, EzyAdmit, Ezyschooling, Uniapply), and Wikipedia for founding/heritage facts. Figures were cross-checked against at least one secondary aggregator where the official site didn't publish a number.

Conflicts & how they were resolved

DAV Public School has two distinct CBSE-affiliated campuses in Patna under the same trust — Khagaul (Cantt Road) and BSEB Colony (Punaichak). This comparison uses the Khagaul campus, which is the one with publicly available teacher/student counts and campus-area data.

Delhi Public School, Patna also operates more than one campus (the senior wing in Danapur Cantonment/Chandmari and the "DPS Patna East" campus on NH-31). Fee figures differ by campus and by publication date — this app cites the DPS Patna East 2025-26 fee circular for the one-time institutional fee, and flags that the full recurring composite fee was not published on the pages we could access. Treat the DPS fee figure as partial, not final.

Sanskriti School, Patna could not be verified. The only well-documented "Sanskriti School" in our research is in Chanakyapuri, New Delhi (est. 1998, run by the Civil Services Society) — a different school entirely. No CBSE school by this name appears in Wikipedia's list of Patna schools or in any admissions aggregator we checked. Rather than silently substitute or invent data, this app keeps the row in the table and flags every cell as unverified. If you meant a different or newer school, tell me its exact name and I'll re-research it.

Board exam pass rates / topper counts were not publicly disclosed in a comparable, citable form for any of the three verified schools at the time of research (school websites and CBSE's own results portal did not surface school-wise aggregate pass percentages for Patna schools). Rather than fabricate a percentage, "Board exam results" is scored as a synthetic placeholder (mid-scale, clearly flagged) reflecting only general reputational mentions in secondary sources — it should carry the least weight in your decision until you can get official numbers directly from each school or CBSE.

Scoring logic

Fees and faculty ratio are normalised 0–10 across the verified schools (lower cost / lower students-per-teacher scores higher). Infrastructure is an analyst judgement based on described facilities and campus size, not a standardised index — treat it as directional. Results is a flagged placeholder, not a real score. Your weight sliders multiply each criterion's score and sum to a single ranking number, recalculated live.
