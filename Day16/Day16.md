# Day 16 – Stock Fundamental Research Skill

## Objective

Create and test a reusable Claude Custom Skill for stock fundamental analysis and company research.

## Skill Created

**Name:** Stock Business Research Analyzer (Educational Version)

## What I Did

* Created a custom Claude Skill for stock research.
* Configured the skill with structured financial analysis instructions.
* Tested the skill using Tata Consultancy Services (TCS).
* Generated a detailed business and financial analysis report.
* Explored company fundamentals, growth metrics, profitability, risks, and competitive position.
* Created a visual summary of the research findings.

## Stock Analyzed

### Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)

### Key Findings

* Strong and consistent revenue growth.
* Healthy profit margins.
* High ROE and ROCE.
* Strong cash generation.
* Very low debt levels.
* Strong global market position in IT services.

### What Surprised Me Most

The most surprising insight was how consistently TCS maintained strong profitability and cash flows despite global economic uncertainty. The analysis also highlighted how low debt and operational efficiency contribute to long-term business strength.

## Key Learnings

1. Custom Skills reduce repetitive prompting.
2. Structured analysis improves research quality.
3. AI can simplify financial statement analysis.
4. Fundamental analysis provides a deeper understanding than stock price movements.
5. Reusable skills increase productivity and consistency.
